NNACT Pro — neutral palette proposal

White and light-grey neutrals; brand yellow fills primary buttons and marks the active item; faint yellow tint plus an amber bar or underline marks selection; status is desaturated, never brand-coloured, and always has an icon and a word. Every pair below is measured against WCAG AA.
